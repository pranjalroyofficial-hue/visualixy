Frequently Asked Questions

Is Visualixy really offline?

Yes. All file operations happen inside your browser. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to download it.

What files can I process?

Images, PDFs, and exam documents are supported. The tool suite includes image resizers, PDF merge, split, compression, conversion and background removal.

How many ads will I see?

Ad triggers are capped in session storage, so direct link popups appear only when processing content and at most once per session or once every three downloads.

Frequently asked questions

Is Visualixy fully local?

Yes. Files are processed in your browser and do not need to be uploaded to a server.

Can I reorder images before making a PDF?

Yes. The image-to-PDF workflow supports drag-free reordering, deletion, and bulk image selection.

Are popunders capped?

Yes. Direct-link popunders are limited to one per tool execution cycle and will not open during normal search or navigation activity.

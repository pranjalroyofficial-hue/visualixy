Frequently asked questions

Is Visualixy fully offline?

Yes. Your files stay in your browser and are processed locally without uploading them to an external server.

Can I compress exam photos under 50KB?

Yes. The SSC and UPSC tool iteratively compresses and rescales to reach tight size targets while preserving aspect ratio.

How are ads balanced?

Direct link popunders only appear after a controlled number of confirm or download actions and are throttled by session storage.

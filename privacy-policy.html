Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 15, 2026 | Official URL: https://visualixy.in/

🔒 Core Privacy Principle: Zero Server File Uploads

Visualixy operates with a privacy-first architecture. All PDF manipulations, image conversions, optical character recognition (OCR), signature optimizations, and invoice calculations are processed 100% locally within your device's memory (RAM) using client-side JavaScript / WebAssembly. Your files, documents, photographs, Aadhaar details, signatures, and personal records are NEVER transmitted, uploaded, or stored on our servers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Because our processing engine is completely client-side:

  • We do not collect or view your uploaded PDF documents, images, or scanned files.
  • We do not store passwords entered during PDF decryption (Unlock PDF).
  • We do not store financial records, invoice items, or identity details created on our tools.

2. Information Automatically Collected (Logs & Analytics)

When you access our web application or mobile app version on the Google Play Store, standard non-personally identifiable diagnostic information may be logged automatically (such as device model, OS version, browser user-agent, and general coarse geographic region) to ensure technical stability, crash resolution, and performance enhancement.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Monetization (AdMob / Ad Networks)

To keep all utilities free of charge, Visualixy displays third-party advertisements (such as Google AdMob, Adsterra, and Monetag). These third-party vendors may use cookies, device advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID), and anonymous identifiers to serve relevant ads according to standard industry guidelines.

Users can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through their Android Device Settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID) or via web browser cookie controls.

4. Permissions Required by the Android Mobile App

Our Android application requests only the minimal permissions required for basic local file picker operations:

  • Read / Write Storage (or Media Picker): Required solely to allow you to select a document from your local storage and save the converted file back to your device.
  • Internet Access: Required to load monetization scripts and software library updates.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Visualixy does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal files or registration details are stored on our servers, our service complies with COPPA and global child privacy regulations.

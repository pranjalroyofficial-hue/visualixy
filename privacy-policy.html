Privacy Policy for Visualixy Web & Mobile App

Last updated: August 10, 2026

1. Overview & Client-Side Processing

Visualixy ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website https://visualixy.in and the Visualixy Android Mobile Application. All document processing (PDF compressing, image resizing, background removal, OCR text extraction, photo cropping) is performed locally in your browser/device RAM using WebAssembly and JavaScript engines. Your uploaded images and documents are NEVER stored, saved, or uploaded to any remote server.

2. Mobile App Hardware Permissions (Camera & Storage)

The Visualixy Mobile Application / APK requests hardware access to the Camera and Device Storage exclusively for the following functionality:

  • Camera Access: Utilized strictly for the live document scanner feature to capture document photos in real-time.
  • Storage Read/Write: Utilized to allow you to select existing photos/PDFs and save processed output files directly to your device downloads folder.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

To keep our suite 100% free, we partner with reputable third-party advertising networks including Adsterra, Monetag, and Google Ads. These networks may collect non-personally identifiable technical information (such as IP address, device model, browser type, and cookie IDs) to serve relevant banner and popunder ads.

4. Cookies & Web Beacons

Visualixy uses essential local cookies to remember user interface preferences. Third-party ad partners may also place cookies on your browser to measure advertisement effectiveness.

5. Children's Privacy

Our tools do not knowingly collect or request personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

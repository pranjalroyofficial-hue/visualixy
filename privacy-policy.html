Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. 100% Client-Side Processing Guarantee

At Visualixy (visualixy.in), privacy is our highest priority. All document utilities (including Image Resizing, PDF Compression, Aadhaar Unlocking, ATS Resume Generation, and OCR scanning) execute strictly client-side inside your browser via HTML5 Canvas, WebAssembly, and JavaScript.

🔒 Zero Server Storage: Your files, photos, aadhaar cards, marksheets, and signatures are never uploaded, stored, or processed on any remote server or database. Once you close or reload the browser tab, all temporary memory is instantly cleared.

2. Information We Collect

Because processing occurs on your device, we do not collect, request, or store sensitive personal information such as passwords, banking credentials, or personal identification numbers.

These partners may use cookies and web beacons to collect non-personally identifiable technical information (such as browser type, time spent, and IP location) to display relevant advertisements.

4. Analytics & Web Performance

We may monitor anonymized technical statistics (e.g., page load speeds, screen dimensions, and aggregate user counts) to optimize performance, mobile responsiveness, and tool load times.

5. Consent & Policy Updates

By using Visualixy tools, you consent to this Privacy Policy. Any updates or changes to our policies will be reflected directly on this page.
